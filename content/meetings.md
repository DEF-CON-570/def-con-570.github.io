## What it costs

Nothing. Meetings are free and there is no membership to sign up for. Show up.

## Your first time

You do not need to prepare anything, know anyone, or work in security. Turn up and sit
down. A few things people usually want to know before a first visit:

- **Do I need to introduce myself?** No. You are welcome to just listen.
- **Do I need a laptop?** No, but it helps if you want to follow along with hands-on
  material.
- **Am I too much of a beginner?** No. A good share of the room learned this stuff at
  meetings like this one.
- **Is it recorded?** Talks may be recorded and posted to our
  [recordings page](/recordings/). The audience is not filmed. If you are presenting
  and would rather not be recorded, just say so. That is fine.
- **Age policy.** There is no age restriction. Everyone is welcome.

## Can I present?

Yes! We would love to have you, and we especially want to hear from first-time
speakers.

**What works well:**

- Anything security, hardware, networking, reverse engineering, privacy, or adjacent.
- A project you built, or one that defeated you. Failure talks are good talks.
- A tool you use constantly that other people have not heard of.
- Something you learned recently. You do not need to be the world expert, only one step
  ahead of the room.

**Practicalities:**

- Slots run about an hour, including questions. Shorter is fine, just tell us what you
  need.
- Slides optional. A terminal and a story is a perfectly good talk.
- We can record it and post it to [recordings](/recordings/), or not. Your call.
