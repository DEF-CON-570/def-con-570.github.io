## Who we are

DEF CON 570 is the official
{{< link dcg "DEF CON Group" >}} for area code
570 in northeastern Pennsylvania. We're volunteer-run and have been meeting year-round
since {{< founded >}}.

We meet on the {{< cadence >}} at {{< venue style="inline" >}}.
See [meetings](/meetings/) for details.

## What we do

- **Talks and demos** on security, hardware, networking, reverse engineering, and
  adjacent subjects.
- **Hands-on sessions**, including lock picking, soldering, radio, and
  capture-the-flag practice.
- **Project help.** Members bring things they're stuck on.
- **Social.** Meeting other people in the area who are into the same things.

## Code of conduct

We follow the official
[DEF CON code of conduct](https://defcon.org/html/links/dc-code-of-conduct.html).

Hack legally. We don't help with, or tolerate, attacks on systems you don't own or have
permission to test.

## Who it is for

Anyone curious, regardless of job title or experience. Students, hobbyists, sysadmins,
developers, people considering a career change, and people who have been doing this for
twenty years all show up.
